### **3. News Widget** 📰
Stay informed with embedded news!

**Features:**
- Fully interactive news sites
- Multiple sources available
- Custom URL support
- Refresh anytime

**How to Use:**
1. Add "News" widget to dashboard
2. Click "Change Source" button
3. Select from popular sources:
   - Google News
   - BBC News
   - CNN
   - Reuters
   - TechCrunch
4. Or enter your own URL!

**Tips:**
- Click, scroll, and navigate within the widget
- Some sites may block embedding (try different sources)
- Refresh button reloads the page

### **4. Forgot Password** 🔐
Reset your password easily!

**How to Use:**
1. On login page, click "Forgot your password?"
2. Enter your email address
3. Click "Send Reset Link"
4. Check your email inbox
5. Click the reset link
6. Set your new password
7. Login with new password!

**Important:**
- Reset link expires in 1 hour
- Check spam folder if you don't see the email
- Link only works once

## 🐛 TROUBLESHOOTING

### **Widgets Not Saving?**
- Make sure you're logged in
- Check internet connection
- Try logout and login again

### **Stocks Not Updating?**
- Check internet connection
- Click manual refresh
- Wait for next auto-update (1 minute)

### **News Not Loading?**
- Some sites block embedding
- Try a different news source
- Check internet connection

### **Forgot Password Email Not Received?**
- Check spam/junk folder
- Wait a few minutes
- Verify email address is correct
- Try again
